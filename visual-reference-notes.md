# Visual Reference Notes from PDF

## Key Design Elements Observed:

1. **Background**: Pastel plaid/gingham pattern (xadrez) in very soft blue, pink, and lavender tones
2. **Color Palette**: 
   - Predominant: Soft blue / baby blue (#B8D4E8 range)
   - Secondary: Soft pink/rose (#E8C4CA range)  
   - Accent: Soft lavender/lilac
   - Background pattern: Very light pastel plaid with blue, pink, and cream
3. **Typography**:
   - Mix of delicate script font ("Psi. Raíssa Alcoforado" in flowing cursive)
   - Bold/strong sans-serif for emphasis ("Raíssa Alcoforado" in bold)
   - Regular readable font for body text
4. **Elements**:
   - Castle illustration (from logo) in soft pastel tones
   - Rainbow in pastel colors
   - Star decorations
   - Circular photo frame for Raíssa
   - QR code for WhatsApp
5. **Overall Feel**: 
   - Soft, delicate but NOT overly baby-ish
   - Professional with warmth
   - The plaid/gingham IS the brand identity
   - Blue is the predominant color (not pink!)
   - Mix of playful and professional typography
6. **Local de Atendimento**: Clínica Crescer – Unidade Cambeba, Av. Viena Weyne, 680
7. **Instagram**: @psi.raissaalcoforado (different from @raissalcoforado?)
